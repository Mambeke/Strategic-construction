OUR PROCESS

A Structured Approach to Every Build.

Every successful construction project begins with proper planning, clear communication and disciplined execution.

FROM FIRST CONVERSATION TO HANDOVER

Seven steps. One clear direction.

The exact process is adapted to the scope and requirements of each project.

01

Initial Consultation

We discuss your objectives, intended use, budget considerations and preferred timing to establish the broad requirements of the project.

02

Site Assessment

We review the site, access, existing conditions and other practical considerations that may affect the scope and sequence of work.

03

Planning and Design

We clarify the scope, coordinate relevant design requirements and consider the resources and construction sequence needed for the work.

04

Quotation and Approval

We prepare a quotation based on the information available, discuss the proposed scope and costs, and seek approval before proceeding.

05

Construction Execution

We coordinate site activities, labour and materials against the agreed scope, keeping the client informed as the work progresses.

06

Quality Control and Inspection

We review workmanship during the project, inspect completed work and identify matters that need attention before handover.

07

Project Handover

We undertake final checks, address agreed outstanding items and hand over the completed work to the client.
