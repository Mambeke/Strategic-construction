Frequently asked questions.

What construction services do you offer?

We provide services across residential, commercial and industrial construction, renovations, civil works, project management, design and build, and maintenance.

Which areas do you serve?

We focus on Cape Town and the wider Western Cape. Please share your project location when enquiring.

How do I request a construction quotation?

Use the project enquiry form to prepare your requirements. Form delivery will be enabled once the company supplies its contact details and a backend is configured.

Can I follow construction progress?

Progress communication can be discussed and agreed as part of the project management approach.

Do you undertake renovations?

Renovations and extensions are among the services described on this website, subject to the specific project scope.

How does the construction process work?

Our process moves from consultation and site assessment through planning, quotation, construction, quality inspection and handover.
